Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Diary is intentionally small and focused. No bloat, no subscriptions, no servers. Just you and your words.

🔒
AES-256 Encryption
Industry-standard encryption protects every entry. Your password is the only key — not stored anywhere.
📴
100% Offline
No internet connection needed, ever. Your journal never leaves your device.
🛡️
Zero Tracking
No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports. Nothing is collected because nothing is sent.
⚡
Lightweight & Fast
Opens instantly. No Electron overhead. Diary is built to be lean and responsive.
🎁
Forever Free
No trial period, no premium tier, no paywalls. Diary is a hobby project made with care — free for everyone.
🔑
Fernet Encryption
Uses Fernet symmetric encryption built on AES-256-CBC. A proven, auditable cryptographic standard.
🖥️
Windows & Linux
Native builds for Windows 10/11 and major Linux distributions. No dependencies required — just download and run.
✍️
Simply Honest
A clean, distraction-free writing experience. Your journal the way it should be.
🔒
Why Diary?

Write freely. No one is watching.

Most journaling apps ask you to trust them with your most private thoughts. Diary removes that trust requirement entirely — because there's simply no server to send your data to.

🏠

Local-first, always

Your entries are files on your own disk. You own them completely.

🤫

Password-based encryption

Your password generates the key. Without it, the encrypted files are meaningless noise.

💜

A hobby project, made with care

No company behind this. No investors. Just a developer who wanted a journal they could trust.
