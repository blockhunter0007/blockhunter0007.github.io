Bot Information & Transparency

This website serves as the official disclosure for the International News Aggregator Bot. Our crawler is designed to collect structured metadata (JSON-LD) to provide high-fidelity, fact-checked news summaries for AI-assisted research.

What our Bot does:

  • Metadata Extraction: We only pull structured data and headlines to ensure accuracy in AI responses.
  • Attribution: Every piece of information is strictly linked back to the original source and gets proper credit.
  • Respecting Robots.txt: We honor Disallow directives and crawl at a low frequency to prevent server strain.
  • Archiving for Verification: Articles are indexed and stored within our system to provide a verifiable "Point of Truth" for AI interactions. Access is provided via our interface or API for research and verification purposes only.

DMCA & Takedown Requests

We respect intellectual property rights. If you believe that your content is being used in a way that constitutes copyright infringement or if you wish to have your site excluded from our index, please follow the instructions below.

Takedown Contact: legal@yourdomain.com

Instructions for Takedown Requests:

  1. Subject Line: Please use "DMCA Takedown Request" or "Crawler Exclusion Request".
  2. Identification: Provide the URL(s) of the content/site in question.
  3. Authority: State your relationship to the content (owner, authorized representative).
  4. Action: Specify if you want a specific article removed or your entire domain blacklisted from our crawler.

As this is a non-profit educational project, requests are typically processed within 48-96 business hours. We appreciate your patience and cooperation in helping us build a more transparent news ecosystem.
