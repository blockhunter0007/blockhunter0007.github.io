Filing a Takedown Notice

To file a DMCA takedown notice, you must provide a written communication containing all of the following elements:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or their authorized agent
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, with sufficient detail to locate the original

⚠ Legal Warning Filing a false or fraudulent DMCA claim is a federal offense. Knowingly submitting a materially false notice may result in personal liability for damages, costs, and legal fees under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).
§ 03
Counter-Notice Procedure

If you believe that content removed pursuant to a DMCA notice was removed erroneously or as a result of misidentification, you may submit a counter-notice. A valid counter-notice must include:

  • Your physical or electronic signature
  • Identification of the removed material and its prior location on the platform
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the content was removed by mistake or misidentification
  • Your name, address, and phone number
  • Consent to jurisdiction of the federal district court in your area

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notice, CheatHub will forward it to the original complainant. If no legal action is initiated within 10–14 business days, the content may be restored at CheatHub's sole discretion.
